# Foldable workspace

## Why

The 500px layout leaves the Fold8 Ultra's inner screen mostly unused. Keeping orders and output visible beside the form reduces scrolling during crafting.

## What Changes

- Show the form and its results in separately scrolling panes when enough width is available.
- Keep the existing stacked flow on narrow screens and preserve drafts while resizing.
- Provide a local preview for testing near the Fold8 Ultra's estimated browser widths.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `responsive-workspace`: Adaptive single-column and two-pane crafting layouts.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Changes the layout of the existing crafting page. No changes to calculations, storage, or production deployment.
